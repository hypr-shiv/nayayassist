Sign in to manage your legal notices with confidence.

NyayaAssist uses Google Firebase strictly for identity authentication. Your uploaded PDFs, OCR text, and court summons remain on your local machine and are never transmitted to Firebase servers.

🔒 Strict Document Isolation

Session cookies are signed with HMAC-SHA256 (HttpOnly) and bound to a non-reversible SHA-256 hash of your Firebase UID.

📋 DPDPA 2023 Informed Consent

Processing never starts before your explicit consent. Automated 24-hour retention shredding applies unless you delete earlier.

🛡️ Zero PII in Server Logs (INV-10)

Your email address, display name, and ID tokens are never written to application logs or audit trails.
